## What's new

Includes 0.5.3, which was merged but not released on its own.

- **Live plan limits after an update:** right after an update (and when you pick **Refresh** in the menu), Brim fetches live Claude and Cursor plan percentages straight away. macOS may ask once for your Keychain; choose **Always Allow** and it won't ask again. Background refreshes never show a password sheet.
- **Fewer password prompts:** Brim no longer re-saves Keychain items in a way that made macOS ask again after updates, and it no longer reads other apps' credentials (Claude Code, `gh`) except when you sign in.
- **Help and feedback:** Settings has a new **Help** page (first open, connections, the island, updates), and **Send Feedback** opens an email with your Brim version filled in. Both are in the menu bar too.
- **More reliable releases:** the release build retries a brief network hiccup instead of failing.

## Install

1. Download **Brim.dmg** below (or use the button in the README).
2. Open it and drag **Brim** to **Applications**.
3. First open: macOS says it can't verify Brim, because it isn't notarized yet. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

Updating from an earlier 0.5 or 0.4: use **Check for Updates** in the app; your Keychain and privacy approvals carry over.

From Terminal (builds on your Mac, no Gatekeeper step):

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhargava14/brim/v0.5.4/install.sh -o install-brim.sh
chmod +x install-brim.sh
./install-brim.sh
```

Verify where the DMG came from (needs the GitHub CLI):

```bash
gh attestation verify Brim.dmg --repo abhargava14/brim
```
