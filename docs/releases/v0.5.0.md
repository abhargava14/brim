## What's new

A calmer, more useful notch: colour only when something needs you, a new Clocks tab, a much better Weather and Calendar, and more ways to fill the closed notch.

- **Clocks (new tab):** your time plus up to three cities, with analog faces, offsets and whether people there are working. Drag the planner to see every clock at another time.
- **Calendar:** Day, Week and Month views, and a legend of your calendars with their accounts. Tap one to hide or show it.
- **Weather:** saved cities as pills, air quality, wind direction, sunrise and sunset, and an hour-by-hour chart for any of the next seven days.
- **AI:** a half-year activity heatmap with your current and best streak.
- **Closed notch:** the next event, the weather and a clock can join the rotation, and you choose how long each item stays. A pinned scratchpad shows its first line.
- **Links:** search with Google, DuckDuckGo, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, YouTube and more, set a default, or add your own engine with a keyword (`yt cats`).
- **Sounds:** night and birdsong, five built-in mixes plus your own saved ones, and your own recorded loops (drop in an audio file).
- **Clipboard:** filters by kind, app icons, colour and email previews, ignored apps (password managers by default), and an opt-in to keep history after quitting.
- **Analytics:** Plausible, Umami (Cloud or self-hosted) and Fathom, next to DataFast and Google Analytics.
- **Files:** Shelf is folded into Files, which converts and shrinks images to JPEG, PNG, HEIC or PDF.
- **Look and feel:** a monochrome island with colour only for warnings, a close button, a hover delay setting and optional haptics.

## Install

1. Download **Brim.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag **Brim** to **Applications**.
3. First open: macOS says it can't verify Brim, because it isn't notarized yet. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

Updating from 0.4.0: use **Check for Updates** in the app; your Keychain and privacy approvals carry over.

From Terminal (builds on your Mac, no Gatekeeper step):

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhargava14/brim/v0.5.0/install.sh -o install-brim.sh
chmod +x install-brim.sh
./install-brim.sh
```

Checksum: see `Brim.dmg.sha256`.

## Privacy

No account and no tracking. Notes, clocks, mixes, screen time and clipboard pins stay on your Mac; clipboard history is off until you turn it on and stays in memory unless you choose to keep it. Weather and air quality come from Open-Meteo and currency rates from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter. Analytics services are contacted only for sites you add.
